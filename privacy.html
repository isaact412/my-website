Privacy Policy

How Carolina Care handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Overview

Carolina Care ("we," "us," "our") operates this website to help patients request medical transportation and to help hospitals and healthcare organizations start a partnership or advertising conversation. This policy explains what information the site collects and how it's used. Carolina Care is currently a demonstration MVP site; it does not have a live backend database, and no accounts or logins exist.

Information We Collect

When you submit a form on this site — a ride request, hospital partnership inquiry, advertising inquiry, or general contact message — we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, phone number, email address, city or ZIP code, appointment details, accessibility needs you choose to share, and your message.

We do not ask for Social Security numbers, FAFSA login credentials, tax documents, insurance identification numbers, diagnoses, or other sensitive medical or financial records through this website.

How Form Submissions Are Handled

Because this website is currently hosted as a static site, form submissions in this version are validated in your browser and stored locally on your own device (via browser local storage) to demonstrate the submission and confirmation experience. They are not currently transmitted to or stored on a Carolina Care server or shared database. A production version of Carolina Care would use a secure backend to route these submissions to Carolina Care coordinators and would be covered by an updated privacy policy.

How We Would Use Information

  • To contact you about a ride request, appointment, or transportation need
  • To follow up on a hospital partnership or advertising inquiry
  • To respond to a general question or message
  • To improve the reliability and usefulness of Carolina Care's services

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Cookies & Local Storage

This site uses your browser's local storage only to demonstrate saving form submissions on this device, and does not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising trackers.

Your Choices

You can clear any information saved in your browser at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this website. You may also contact us to ask what information we hold about you or to request that it be deleted.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be directed to Carolina Care at 1-800-CALL-CARE or through the Contact page.

Carolina Care is a non-emergency transportation concept. For medical emergencies, call 911.
