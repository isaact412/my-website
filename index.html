How Carolina Care works

You need care.

You may live hours from the specialist or hospital you need.

We get you there.

Carolina Care coordinates affordable medical transportation.

Hospitals help fund the ride.

Hospital partnerships and other revenue sources allow Carolina Care to keep patient costs low.

How it works

From a phone call to the hospital door

Four simple steps — no smartphone or internet connection required.

1

Call or Request

Patients call 1-800-CALL-CARE or submit the online ride request.

2

We Coordinate

Carolina Care confirms pickup location, hospital, appointment, travel time, and available route.

3

We Pick You Up

A Carolina Care vehicle arrives at the scheduled pickup location. Riders traveling the same direction may share routes to reduce cost.

4

You Reach Care

Patients arrive at the appropriate hospital or specialty center and receive return transportation on their scheduled trip.

Who we serve

Built for patients distance leaves behind

Rural families

Households far from advanced medical centers who need a dependable way to get to care.

Older adults

Patients who no longer drive long distances or who need extra support getting to appointments.

Patients without reliable vehicles

People who don't have a car, or whose only vehicle isn't available when a ride is needed.

Specialist appointments

Patients traveling for cardiology, oncology, or other specialty care not available nearby.

Recurring-care patients

Dialysis, chemotherapy, and physical therapy patients who need consistent, repeated trips.

Patients far from major hospitals

Communities located an hour or more from the nearest advanced hospital or medical center.

Carolina Care is not an ambulance or emergency transportation service. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911.

How it's funded

A sustainable model, not a donation drive

Hospitals help fund transportation because improving access can help patients reach care earlier. Healthcare advertising provides another revenue source, while patients who can afford to contribute may pay an income-based fare.

Hospital Partnerships

Major hospitals contract with Carolina Care to improve transportation access for patients who live far from advanced care.

Healthcare Advertising

Healthcare and medical companies can sponsor appropriate advertising inside Carolina Care vehicles.

Income-Based Fares

Patients who can reasonably contribute may pay an income-based fare, while lower-income patients may receive highly subsidized transportation.
