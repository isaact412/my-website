About Carolina Care

Distance Shouldn't Determine Access to Care

Carolina Care was designed around a simple problem: advanced healthcare may exist, but patients still need a way to reach it.

By partnering with hospitals and building efficient transportation routes, Carolina Care connects rural communities with specialty care while creating a sustainable business model around transportation access.

Mission

Make distance a smaller barrier to healthcare.

Every patient deserves a realistic way to reach the hospital or specialist their care depends on — regardless of what's parked in their driveway.

Model

Hospital-supported transportation.

Funded through hospital partnerships, with additional revenue from healthcare sponsorships and income-based patient fares.

Approach

Simple, dependable, phone-accessible.

A scheduled ride, coordinated by a real person, that shows up when and where it's supposed to.

Built for patients without reliable internet or smartphones

The original Carolina Care concept has to work for patients who don't have dependable electricity, a smartphone, or an internet connection. That's why the phone stays the primary way to reach us — 1-800-CALL-CARE. This website is an additional channel, not the only way to access Carolina Care. Vehicles run on conventional fuel, not a charging network, so service doesn't depend on infrastructure many rural communities don't have.
