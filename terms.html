About This Site

This website describes Carolina Care, a proposed medical transportation service for rural and underserved South Carolina communities, and is currently presented as a demonstration MVP (minimum viable product). By using this site, you agree to these terms.

Not an Emergency Service

Carolina Care is not an ambulance service and does not provide emergency medical transportation. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 immediately. Nothing on this site should be relied on for emergency situations.

Demonstration Content

Some content on this site — including the Route Demo, sample cost estimates, and illustrative figures — is presented for demonstration purposes only. It does not represent real patients, real hospital partnerships, real operational data, or guaranteed pricing. Where figures are hypothetical, they are labeled as a demonstration, example, projection, or illustration.

No Guarantee of Service

Submitting a ride request through this website is a request for transportation, not a guarantee of immediate availability. Trip confirmation, scheduling, and pricing are determined by a Carolina Care coordinator.

No Medical Advice

Carolina Care provides transportation coordination only. Nothing on this website constitutes medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always follow the guidance of your healthcare provider.

Accounts & Payments

This website does not require account creation, does not collect passwords, and does not process payments. Any pricing shown (including the example cost calculator) is illustrative only.

Changes to These Terms

Carolina Care may update these terms as the service develops. Continued use of the site after changes are posted means you accept the updated terms.

Contact
