For Hospitals

Extend Your Care Beyond the Hospital

Carolina Care helps healthcare systems connect rural patients with specialty care through reliable transportation partnerships.

Partner With Carolina Care Call 1-800-CALL-CARE
The problem

Distance is a barrier your patients can't schedule around

Transportation barriers may contribute to:

  • Missed appointments
  • Delayed treatment
  • Interrupted follow-up care
  • Preventable deterioration
  • Avoidable high-cost care
  • Poorer patient experiences

Carolina Care helps close the distance between the patient and the hospital.

The solution

A simple transportation loop, built around your patients

Hospital identifies transportation need
↓
Carolina Care coordinates patient route
↓
Patient receives transportation
↓
Hospital expands access to its care network

Hospital partnerships can be structured through regional service agreements, transportation contracts, patient transportation programs, or sponsored routes — tailored to each health system's service area.
